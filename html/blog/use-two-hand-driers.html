Use Two Hand Driers

You can use two hand driers at once, one per hand, to speed up drying time:

  • You save time by drying both hands in parallel.
  • You save time because the dryer doesn’t redistribute water from one hand to the other, which happens when both hands are dried at the same time on the same drier.
